ZapFast 0.22.0 adds a privacy screen: one shortcut hides names, pictures and messages from people looking at your screen, and pointing at something shows it.

## New

- **A privacy screen for cafés, offices and shared screens.** `Ctrl+Shift+P` (`⌘⇧P` on macOS) turns it on and off. Chat names, profile pictures, chat-list previews, search results, starred messages, the open chat's messages and the text you are typing are drawn as soft bars until you point at them, and notifications say only "New message" or "Incoming call". It is never saved, so ZapFast always starts with it off. It hides content from passers-by; it does not lock ZapFast, which the app lock does. (#1, #2)

## Known limitations

- The privacy screen does not yet cover the info panel, the Calls page, reactions, reply strips, typing bubbles or the image preview.
- Calls are still a work in progress, as described in 0.20.0: video calls are in alpha, and in group calls your video stops for a moment every few seconds.
